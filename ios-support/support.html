Pactime support

Need a hand with a limit, a schedule or a purchase? Email vergilluw@gmail.com. Include “Pactime” in the subject, your iOS and app versions, and what happened.

Start with an app limit

Open Apps, add a daily limit, allow Screen Time access and choose an app. Set the time you want to allow each day. Earlier usage in the current cycle counts toward the limit.

When time runs out

iOS displays a restriction screen when you open the app. Its Home Screen icon may dim. Access returns when the applicable limits or schedules end. If more than one restriction applies, each must end before the app is available.

Changing a limit

Lower limits apply immediately. With Free, increases and removal take effect at that rule’s next daily reset. Pro allows immediate changes. Time already used still counts. Free rules reset at midnight; Pro can use a custom reset time.

Restore Pro or manage a subscription

Open My → Pactime Pro → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Each device keeps its own rules. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription in your Apple account settings. Cancel a free trial at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid the next charge. Refund requests are handled through Apple’s purchase support.

Permission and enforcement

Pactime relies on Apple’s Screen Time permission and system scheduling. Check the permission in Settings if restrictions stop working. Reopen Pactime to inspect the affected rule and any pending changes. Let support know the expected start/end time and when the restriction actually appeared.

Read the privacy policy and Apple standard Terms of Use.

Pactime 使用帮助

遇到限额、定时计划或购买问题，可以发邮件到 vergilluw@gmail.com。标题写上「Pactime」，并说明 iOS 版本、应用版本和遇到的情况，方便我们排查。

设置第一个限额

进入「应用」，添加每日限额，允许访问屏幕使用时间并选择应用，然后设置每天可以使用的时长。当前周期此前已经使用的时间也会计入。

额度用完后会怎样

再次打开应用时，iOS 会显示限制页面，桌面上的应用图标也可能变灰。相关限额或计划结束后恢复使用。同一个应用如果同时受到多项限制，需要等这些限制都结束。

修改限额什么时候生效

减少时长立即生效。免费版增加时长或移除限额，在该规则下次每日重置时生效；Pro 可以立即生效。已经使用的时间仍会计入。免费版在每天凌晨重置，Pro 可以选择其他重置时间。

恢复会员与管理订阅

使用购买时的 Apple 账户，进入「我的 → Pactime Pro → 恢复购买」。各台设备的规则分别保存在本机。你可以在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。如不打算继续试用后的订阅，请至少在试用结束前 24 小时取消。退款申请可通过 Apple 购买支持提交。

权限与限制异常

Pactime 依赖 Apple 的屏幕使用时间权限与系统调度。限制失效时，请先在系统设置中检查权限，再回到 Pactime 查看相关规则及待生效修改。联系客服时，请说明设置的开始、结束时间和实际发生限制的时间。
