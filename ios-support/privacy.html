Privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026 · Pactime for iPhone

Pactime helps you set app limits and focus schedules on your iPhone. Vergil Lu is responsible for Pactime and can be contacted at vergilluw@gmail.com.

Screen Time and your local settings

With your authorization, Pactime uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks to let you choose apps, set limits, schedule restrictions and display usage reports. App selections, opaque app tokens, limits, schedules, scenes, preferences and restriction-screen settings are processed and stored on your device. App extensions share the settings needed to apply restrictions through a local App Group.

Pactime’s app-management features operate locally. Screen Time authorization is managed by iOS, and you can revoke it in Settings. Revoking access stops restrictions while keeping your saved settings.

Images you choose

The system photo picker lets you select a reminder image. Pactime processes that image on your device, reduces it to a small PNG and stores the processed version for the restriction screen. You can replace or remove it in My → Restriction screen.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases, introductory offers, subscription billing, refunds and restoration through the App Store. Pactime reads Apple-verified transaction and subscription status to determine your Pro access. Apple manages your payment information and its own transaction records under Apple’s privacy policy.

Support requests

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as your app version and a description of the problem. We use it to respond and investigate. Email is handled by the email service providers used by you and the support inbox.

Retention and deletion

Local rules and preferences remain until you change or remove them, or delete Pactime from the device. Removing the app removes its local app data from that device. Apple manages device backups and system diagnostics through your device and account settings. Manage any backups separately in those settings.

We retain support correspondence while handling your request and necessary follow-up. You can email support to request deletion of your correspondence, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligation. Apple retains its own purchase records under its policies. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings, including after deleting Pactime.

Third parties and changes

Apple provides Screen Time, photo selection, App Store payments and operating-system services. Any future change that adds an external service or changes how Pactime handles personal information will be reflected in this policy and, where required, in the app’s consent flow. The date above identifies the current version.

隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 12 日 · Pactime iPhone 版

Pactime 帮你在 iPhone 上设置应用限额与专注计划。本应用由 Vergil Lu 负责，联系邮箱为 vergilluw@gmail.com。

屏幕使用时间与本机设置

获得你的授权后，Pactime 通过 Apple 的屏幕使用时间框架选择应用、设置限额、安排限制时段并展示用量报告。应用选择、用于识别应用的系统令牌、限额、定时计划、常用场景、偏好和限制页面设置在本机处理与保存。应用与系统扩展通过本地 App Group 共享执行限制所需的设置。

应用管理功能在本机运行。屏幕使用时间权限由 iOS 管理，你可以在系统设置中撤销。撤销后，限制会停止，已保存的设置会保留。

你选择的图片

系统照片选择器允许你主动选择一张提醒图片。Pactime 会在本机缩小并转为 PNG，保存处理后的图片供限制页面使用。你可以在「我的 → 限制页面」中更换或移除。

购买与订阅

购买、首次优惠、订阅扣款、退款和恢复购买由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。Pactime 读取经过 Apple 核验的交易与订阅状态，以判断 Pro 权益。付款资料及 Apple 的交易记录由 Apple 按其隐私政策管理。

联系客服

你发送客服邮件时，我们会收到邮箱地址以及你主动提供的信息，例如应用版本与问题描述。这些信息用于回复和排查问题。邮件由你使用的邮件服务商及客服收件箱的邮件服务商处理。

数据保留与删除

本机规则与偏好会保留到你修改、移除，或从设备中删除 Pactime。删除应用会移除这台设备上的应用本地数据。设备备份与系统诊断由 Apple 的系统和账户设置管理，请在相关设置中单独管理备份。

客服邮件在处理咨询和必要后续沟通期间保留。你可以通过邮件要求删除相关咨询记录；依法需要保留的记录按适用要求处理。Apple 的购买记录由其依据自身政策保留。订阅可在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消，删除 Pactime 后仍需单独管理订阅。

第三方与政策更新

屏幕使用时间、照片选择、App Store 付款和操作系统服务由 Apple 提供。后续如加入外部服务或改变个人信息处理方式，我们会更新本政策，并在需要时通过应用征求同意。页面上方日期用于标明当前版本。
