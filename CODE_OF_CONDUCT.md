# Community code of conduct

Pactime welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

- Discuss the work and its impact; do not attack the person.
- Respect differences in language, ability, and technical experience.
- Give actionable feedback, credit others, and accept reasonable corrections.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, spam, or publish someone else's private information.
- Do not use self-control or accessibility difficulties as a reason to blame or shame someone reporting a problem.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate material, ask for changes in behavior, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should consider context, impact, and the opportunity to correct a mistake.

For a concern, contact a maintainer through a private contact they have made available on their GitHub profile. If no channel is available, request one without publishing personal or sensitive details. Urgent security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

请尊重不同背景与经验的参与者，就事论事，不人身攻击、不骚扰、不泄露隐私。反馈产品问题时，不应因自律、无障碍或使用习惯责备用户。维护者可根据情况移除不当内容或限制参与。
